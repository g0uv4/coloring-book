# Analysis method

Do this **before** calling the image tool. Do not generate from a vibe.

## Mapping rule

Every line you plan to generate must map to a named fact above. If a planned flower wreath is not in the photo and not needed to close a composition, drop it. A simple ground line or sun is allowed only when the photo already has ground or sky.

## Kind shortcuts

- **portrait:** one person, face is the page. Spend most of the line budget on hair clumps + glasses + clothes blocks. Background almost empty.
- **group:** preserve count and height order. Slightly simpler faces than a solo portrait.
- **pet:** species silhouette first, then collar/bow. Eyes simple.
- **scene:** 5–10 big objects, not a tourist postcard etching.
- **object:** one hero object, optional 2 supporting objects, lots of white.

## Stop conditions

- Photo is unreadable / too dark / too tiny → ask for a clearer upload
- Photo is a screenshot of a copyrighted character model sheet → refuse to redraw official art; offer to stylize a real photograph instead
- Photo is sexual content involving a minor → refuse
