# Portrait rules

Faces are where photo-to-line conversions fail. Either they become a traced photograph (too much) or a stick figure (too little). Open-Line Plate sits in the middle: a **recognizable cartoon of this person**. Intensity changes how many extra seams you add around the face, never the core recipe.

## Must keep (every intensity)

- Apparent age band (child / teen / adult / elder) without adding makeup or aging
- Hair silhouette: length, bangs, bun, baldness, side part — as closed clumps
- Glasses: two lenses + bridge + arms. Round stays round, rectangle stays rectangle
- Facial hair as one or two masses (beard strap, mustache banana), not stubble dots
- Distinctive marks only if large and identity-critical (a beauty mark, a scar) as a tiny simple shape — skip faint freckle fields
- Skin tone is **not drawn**. It is a white region the user will color
- Clothing identity: a striped shirt stays striped; a logo becomes a simple geometric badge, never a readable trademark lockup

## Face recipe (use this every time)

- Head: simple oval / egg, slightly oversized vs photoreal (coloring-book canon)
- Eyes: almond or oval outline + iris circle + pupil. Optional tiny highlight circle. No individual eyelashes. No eyelid crease stacks
- Brows: one curved stroke each
- Nose: a small U, L, or button. No nostril caves, no photoreal planes
- Mouth: one curve, or a simple closed lip outline. Teeth only as a single rounded rectangle if the person is clearly laughing with teeth shown — never a full dental chart
- Ears: C-shape, no inner-ear maze
- Neck: two short lines. No tendon detail

## Intensity around the face

| | simple | medium | advanced |
|---|---|---|---|
| Hair clumps | 3–5 | 4–8 | 8–12, still clumps |
| Glasses | two circles + bridge | + arms | + temples and a simple frame thickness |
| Clothes | 1 silhouette | 1–3 blocks, 2–5 stripes | extra collar / pocket / panel lines |
| Still forbidden | lashes, pores, wrinkles, strand hair | same | same |

## Body recipe

- Hands: mitten or four-finger cartoon. No knuckle wrinkles, no fingernails unless a ring is identity-critical
- Fingers: if the photo shows a clear gesture (peace sign, pointing, holding a cup), keep the gesture with simplified digits
- Full body: bean-simple torso, simplified shoes as 2–3 shapes (advanced may add a sole line or laces as 2–3 loops)
- Headshot: stop at upper chest. Do not invent a full body
- Group: keep left-to-right order and relative heights

## Children

Ordinary family photos of children are in scope. Draw them with the same recipe, slightly larger heads, simpler hands. Never sexualize. Never add a more adult body. Prefer **simple** or **medium** unless the user asked for advanced.

## Groups

- Preserve count. Do not drop a person in the back row
- Overlaps stay overlaps (a child in front of an adult)
- Faces further back may be even simpler, but still have eyes / mouth

## Failures to reject

- Photoreal "ink drawing" of the face (this is the most common model failure)
- Gray skin shading
- Hair as thousands of lines
- Empty oval with dots for eyes (too little)
- Beautified celebrity version that no longer matches the photo
