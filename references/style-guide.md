# Open-Line Plate — style law

One locked **line language** for every photo. Density is chosen from three intensities (`simple` / `medium` / `advanced`) — see [intensity.md](intensity.md). Do not invent extra styles (watercolor, manga tone, mandala, photoreal ink).

## What it is

Rebuild the photograph as a **coloring-book illustration**. The page must look like it was inked for crayons or markers, not like a photo with the color sucked out.

## Ink and paper

- Background: pure white `#FFFFFF`. No paper grain, no gradient, no beige wash.
- Lines: near-black ink `#111111`–`#000000`. One consistent weight per plate (thicker for simple; medium and advanced share the same medium felt-tip). No taper-to-hairline filigree.
- Interiors: empty white, so a crayon can fill them.
- The only allowed solid black fills are tiny: pupils, a small nose button, a belt buckle, a button. Never fill hair, skin, clothes, sky, or water.

## Shape language

- Closed regions. Gaps that leak into neighboring areas are a defect.
- Region size scales with intensity (see intensity.md). Even advanced plates must have pockets a fine marker can land in.
- Line budget: the whole page should read clearly at 15 cm tall. If a detail vanishes at that size, drop it (or it belongs only on advanced, and only if it is a named structural seam).
- Corners may be slightly rounded. Avoid mechanical CAD look and avoid shaky scan-tracer look.

## What survives from the photo

Keep relational identity:

- Who is there (count of people and animals)
- Body crop and pose
- Haircut silhouette, facial hair as a simple mass, glasses, hats
- Clothing blocks (count of seams scales with intensity)
- Distinctive props (a specific bag, a landmark roof, a car type)
- Background masses (count scales with intensity)

Discard photographic surface at every intensity: pores, individual strands, grass blades, brick-by-brick, fabric weave, specular skin, makeup texture, noise, EXIF dates, UI chrome, watermarks.

## Density cap (non-negotiable, including advanced)

Photo conversion is **not** adult mandala coloring.

Forbidden density:

- Zentangle fills inside ears, petals, clothes
- Hundreds of leaf veins
- Cross-hatching, stipple, scribble shade
- Photoreal hands with every knuckle wrinkle
- City scenes with every window pane
- Heat-shield or brick drawn tile-by-tile

Allowed supporting decoration, if it already exists in the photo: a few large flowers, a simple fence, a sun with short rays, a banner of outline letters. Advanced may add extra petals or fence posts, still as closed shapes.

## Colorability test

Before delivery, imagine handing the page to someone with a 12-color crayon box (simple/medium) or a 12-color fine marker set (advanced). If they would not know where yellow vs blue goes because regions are shredded into specks, the plate is too complex. Simplify and regenerate.

## Reference role

Style is **text-only**. This pack ships **no** coloring plates. Do not download, bundle, or feed third-party coloring pages into the image model. If the user attached examples, they taught the *author* of this skill; they are not assets and not IMAGE 1.
