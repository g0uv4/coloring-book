# Style reference index

Four original plates. They define **ink, closure, and density** — not subject matter.

Feed **one** of them as IMAGE 1 (style). Never paste their subjects onto the user's photo.

| File | Use when the photo is… | What to steal | What to ignore |
|---|---|---|---|
| `assets/style-references/01-portrait.png` | people, selfies, family, half-body, groups | Face recipe, hair clumps, clothing blocks, simple ground | The two waving children |
| `assets/style-references/02-scene.png` | outdoor places, travel, beach, street, landscape | Big object outlines, simple water/sky, floral as large petals | The SUMMER sign and surfboard |
| `assets/style-references/03-pet.png` | cats, dogs, rabbits, animals with a bit of greenery | Animal silhouette, large petals, white space | That specific cat |
| `assets/style-references/04-objects.png` | food, still life, product shots, scattered objects | Isolated closed objects, generous gaps | The Summer banner and those props |

If the photo mixes kinds (a person holding a dog on a beach), pick the **primary subject**: the thing the user would color first. Portrait beats pet beats scene beats objects.

## Density reminder

These plates are the **ceiling**. Do not go denser than `01-portrait.png`. Do not fill regions with mandala texture even if the user attached a mandala as inspiration — that inspiration is "coloring book", not "copy this filigree onto my face".
